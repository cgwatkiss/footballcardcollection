Quick start

How to play

  1. Open packs with coins to collect player cards.
  2. Choose up to 11 owned players for your squad.
  3. Battle a generated opponent; your squad rating and chemistry-free team strength determine the result.
  4. Sell duplicates for coins or keep them for your collection.
